# Repository Artefacts

ClickUp stores delivery work. The repository stores durable product and technical evidence.

Recommended paths:

- `docs/product/prds/` — approved PRDs
- `docs/product/audits/` — current-state and product audits
- `docs/research/` — research plans and evidence
- `docs/business/` — processes, rules, permissions, glossary, data dictionary
- `docs/design/` — design handoffs and linked design references
- `docs/architecture/adr/` — architecture decision records
- `docs/architecture/` — architecture diagrams and contracts
- `docs/security/reviews/` — threat models and security reports
- `docs/quality/test-plans/` — risk-based plans
- `docs/quality/release-evidence/` — verification summaries
- `docs/operations/runbooks/` — deployment, rollback, incident, and recovery procedures
- `docs/operations/incidents/` — incident records
- `docs/release/` — release plans and notes
- `docs/decisions/` — cross-functional decision log
- `docs/delivery/goals/` — Build, Stage, task, and verification Goal Contracts and iteration evidence
- `docs/delivery/BUILD_STATE.md` — lightweight pointer to ClickUp Build Control, active goals, and last gate only

Do not create `docs/product/tickets/` or duplicate ClickUp descriptions in Markdown.
