## Description (draft)
SelahCue Controller turns your phone or tablet into a secure remote for a SelahCue desktop on your
church's local network. Advance slides and song lyrics, go live, black out, drive countdown timers,
search and stage scripture, approve auto-detected verses, and watch the live transcript — all with
server-enforced roles so volunteers only see the controls they're meant to use.

- **Offline-first & private:** connects only to the desktop you pair with, over an encrypted,
  certificate-pinned local-network link. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
- **Secure pairing:** scan a QR from the desktop; an operator confirms your device and assigns a role.
- **Role-aware:** the desktop stays in control; losing your phone never disrupts the live service.
