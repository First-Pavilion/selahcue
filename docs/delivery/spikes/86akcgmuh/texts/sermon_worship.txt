In the book of Revelation, John is given a glimpse into the throne room of heaven, and do you know what he hears? He hears the living creatures crying out day and night, never resting, Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was and who is and who is to come. They have been saying it since before the mountains were formed, and they have never once grown bored. Think about that. Whatever they are seeing when they look at God, it is so overwhelming that every time they look again they discover something new to be astonished by. That is what worship is meant to be. Not a warm up act before the sermon. Not a few songs while the late arrivals find their seats. Worship is the moment when the people of God lift their eyes off themselves and look at him long enough to be astonished again. So when the band starts to play next Sunday, I want to challenge you. Do not just sing the words on the screen. Look at the one the words are about. Remember what he has carried you through this year. Remember the hospital room and the job that fell through and the phone call that came just in time. And then sing like somebody who has actually been rescued, because you have.
