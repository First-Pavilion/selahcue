He is faithful when the harvest is plentiful, and he is faithful when the field is bare. He is faithful when the doctor calls with good news, and he is faithful when the waiting room is cold and quiet and nobody will look you in the eye. I have walked with this congregation long enough to know that some of you came in today carrying something heavy. Maybe it is a marriage that feels like two strangers sharing a house. Maybe it is a son who has stopped answering the phone. Maybe it is a diagnosis that you have not told anyone about yet, not even the people sitting beside you right now. I am not going to stand up here and pretend that faith makes every road smooth. The psalmist did not pretend that either. He wrote about the valley of the shadow of death, and he did not say we would go around it. He said we would walk through it. Through it. And he said, I will fear no evil, for you are with me. Notice where the comfort comes from. It does not come from the valley getting shorter or the shadows getting lighter. It comes from the one who walks beside us. So hold on to that this week. When the night is long, remember who is keeping watch. When the answer is slow, remember who is writing the story.
