## 1. Product vision

Churches buy SelahCue once, activate it on the machines they own, and then run Sunday after Sunday — including fully offline — without licensing ever getting in their way. The platform is the commercial spine behind that: accounts, licence keys, device instances, signed offline entitlements, renewal, distribution and staff operations. It exists to make the business viable **without ever compromising the product's foundational promise: no licensing state, licensing outage, or licensing job may blank, alter or block live output during a service** (desktop PRD NFR-024, NFR-015, CON-2).

## 2. Problem statement

The platform was built ahead of its requirements. The API ships licence-key issuance, device activation, `license:refresh`, and an Ed25519-signed offline entitlement manifest — but **every state transition after first activation is absent**. `LicenseKeyStatus` declares 8 values (`apps/license_keys/models.py:13-21`); only `ISSUED` and `ACTIVATED` are ever written (`apps/license_keys/services.py:135`, `apps/devices/services.py:443-444`). `EXPIRED`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, `CONVERTED` and `ARCHIVED` have no writer anywhere; `EXPIRING` appears exactly once, as a *read*, in the activatable set (`apps/devices/services.py:40`). `DeviceStatus.ACTIVE` is the only device status ever assigned (`apps/devices/models.py:35`) — `DeviceStatus.REVOKED` is consumed (`apps/devices/services.py:130,587`) but never produced. The hourly revocation cascade (`apps/devices/tasks.py`) therefore consumes licence states nothing produces. The desktop consumes none of this — no activation, entitlement or licence code exists in any of the 12 Rust crates, and no ed25519 dependency is in `implementation/desktop/Cargo.lock` — so nothing is currently enforceable end-to-end. Meanwhile `downloads:prepare`, `downloads/<lease>:complete`, `usage-events:batch` (`selahcue_api/platform/urls.py`) and the billing webhook (`selahcue_api/urls.py`) are 501 stubs, and `apps/billing`, `apps/catalogue`, `apps/downloads` are empty scaffolds. Without a PRD, none of this can be finished, verified, or safely enforced.

## 3. Personas and roles

- **Church administrator (buyer).** Purchases and renews; owns the org account; manages which machines are activated; non-technical.
- **Tech director / operator.** Installs and activates the desktop on sanctuary machines; replaces hardware; must never be blocked on a Sunday.
- **Volunteer operator.** Runs services; should never see licensing at all unless something needs renewing.
- **SelahCue staff administrator.** Issues, suspends, revokes, reinstates and converts licences; investigates abuse; needs full audit history.
- **SelahCue support.** Diagnoses "my licence doesn't work" with read access and audit trails; must be able to see what the customer sees.
- **SelahCue finance/ops.** Owns renewal, dunning and refunds once D1/D2 are decided.

## 4. Jobs to be done

- JTBD-P1: "Buy SelahCue and be presenting on our machines the same day, without a support ticket."
- JTBD-P2: "Run every service — including in a building with no internet — without licensing ever interrupting us."
- JTBD-P3: "Replace a dead presentation machine on Saturday night and be live Sunday morning."
- JTBD-P4: "Know exactly what we own: which machines are activated, when we expire, what renewing costs."
- JTBD-P5 (staff): "Kill a leaked or fraudulent key quickly, and prove afterwards exactly what happened and when."
- JTBD-P6 (staff): "Suspend a non-paying account without silently ruining their Sunday" (severity decided 2026-08-25: soft suspend + session-boundary downgrade — D3/D4, §27).

## 5. Goals

**"Licensing works" means, testably:** a church can (a) create an account and buy/receive a licence, (b) activate the desktop on up to its instance limit of machines, (c) run services fully offline for the whole licence window, (d) deactivate and replace a machine themselves, (e) see their own licence, devices and expiry, and (f) renew — while (g) staff can drive every one of the 8 licence statuses through an audited writer, and (h) **at no point does any licensing state, API response, outage or scheduled job blank, alter or block the live audience output or core presentation controls during a running session** (NFR-024/NFR-015/CON-2). Completion is: every status reachable, every transition audited, every enforcement behaviour in §13 EPIC-PL-C demonstrated by test, and METRIC-501…505 instrumented.

- G-P1: Every licence and device status is reachable, observable and enforced as specified (no dead states).
- G-P2: Licensing is invisible on Sunday: enforcement acts between sessions and on non-core surfaces only.
- G-P3: Customers self-serve activation, deactivation, replacement and visibility without support tickets.
- G-P4: Staff operate the full lifecycle with a complete audit trail.
- G-P5: Owner decisions are surfaced with consequences so the owner can close them; nothing in this PRD decides them. As of v1.2 the owner has closed D3–D6 and part of D1 (§27; DEC-008…DEC-012, with the rationale carried into §13); the D1 remainder and D2 stay surfaced as open.

The 6 affiliate views in `implementation/marketing` remain presentation-only shells; no affiliate requirements exist in this PRD. Payout accuracy metrics ride with it.
- **NG-P2: Any licensing enforcement that can blank, degrade or block live output or core presentation controls mid-session.** Enforcement is launch-time and non-core-surface only. (The Free-tier watermark decided under D1 is a constant, declared property of the tier — FR-548 — evaluated at session start; it is not an enforcement action and may never be applied, removed or altered mid-session as a reaction to a licensing state change; FR-549 extends this same session-boundary rule to every dimension of a downgrade.)
- **NG-P3: Cloud-hosted presentation control.** The platform is licensing/commerce/distribution; the desktop remains authoritative (PRD v1.1 NG-5). No platform surface may issue presentation commands.
- **NG-P4: Per-user seat licensing.** The licensed unit is the account-bound device instance (DEC-004). The D1 tier table's "seats" are exactly these licensed device instances under the org's single licence key — "each with its own license" means each seat's own activation/device token, not its own key (AS-P8) — not per-user seats; `seat_limit` (per-user) exists in the schema but stays dormant (DEC-007 product answers); enforcing it is a separate future decision.
- **NG-P5: BYOK / user-supplied AI keys** (Providers & Privacy decision: hosted-only) and **TTS**, which remains removed from the roadmap (DEC-001) — restated here so no platform surface reintroduces either.
- **NG-P6: Building billing/payment integration before D1 (commercial model) and D2 (provider/merchant-of-record) are decided.** The webhook stub stays a stub until then.
- **NG-P7: 2FA for customer accounts** — explicitly deferred by DEC-007 (forward-compatible seam only).
- **NG-P8: Hardware dongles, node-locking beyond the existing device fingerprint, or online-only DRM.** Offline-first is a product promise (CON-2).

## 8. Constraints

- **CON-P1 (inherits NFR-024):** No licensing failure, state, or job may blank/alter/block live output. Every requirement in §13 is subordinate to this.
- **CON-P2 (inherits NFR-015/CON-2):** Core presentation runs with zero network for the entire entitlement window. Licensing checks never sit on the render or live-control path.
- **CON-P3:** Desktop-authoritative (NG-5): the platform can refuse *future* API service, never command the running app.
- **CON-P4:** Owner decisions are owner-owned. D3–D6 and part of D1 are decided (2026-08-25; DEC-008…DEC-012) and folded into the requirements; requirements still gated on the D1 remainder or D2 stay marked DEFERRED and must not be built ahead of the decision.
- **CON-P5:** All platform DB changes remain forward-only additive migrations; licensing writers must be idempotent and bounded (the shipped cascade's `batch_size` pattern, `apps/devices/tasks.py:36-41`).
- **CON-P6:** No user-enumeration oracle on any public surface; safe coded errors only (`graphql/errors.py`). The one deliberate exception is decided (D6, 2026-08-25 — DEC-012): a distinct EXPIRED code on authenticated licence/entitlement surfaces only (FR-523); the auth-token collapse stands (FR-529).
- **CON-P7:** The signed entitlement envelope's signature covers the transmitted base64url payload exactly (`apps/entitlements/signing.py` header comment); clients must never re-serialize before verifying.

## 10. Competitor and comparable licensing models (summary)

Inferred from public product behaviour (not re-researched for this PRD; product-researcher can deepen on request): **ProPresenter** sells seat-count licences where an expired licence keeps *presenting* but loses updates/support — expiry never bricks a service. **EasyWorship/MediaShout** similarly degrade to non-updating but functional. **Planning Center** is subscription-SaaS (hard-stops on lapse) but is not in the live-output path. The comparable-product norm for live-production software is clear: **lapsed licences degrade commercial surfaces, not the show**. This norm informed D4, which the owner closed on 2026-08-25 consistent with it (§27; DEC-009).

## 13. Functional requirements

### The licence lifecycle state machine (normative)

This is the core deliverable. Product semantics for every status; the writer column is the requirement — today six of eight licence statuses have none.

**`LicenseKeyStatus`** (`apps/license_keys/models.py:13-21`). "Activatable" = in `ACTIVATABLE_KEY_STATUSES` (`apps/devices/services.py:40`): ISSUED, ACTIVATED, EXPIRING.

| Status | Meaning | Writer (today → required) | Customer sees | Device effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ISSUED | Key exists, never activated | Shipped: staff issuance (`services.py:135`) | "Ready to activate" + instructions | None yet; activation permitted |
| ACTIVATED | ≥1 device activated | Shipped: first activation (`devices/services.py:443-444`) | Licence active, window dates, devices used/limit | Full service; activation up to limit |
| EXPIRING | Inside the renewal-notice window before `expires_at` | **None → FR-502** (scheduled) | Renewal banner + emails with renew path | Unchanged — still activatable by design |
| EXPIRED | `now > expires_at` | **None → FR-503** (scheduled) | "Expired on {date}" + renew path + grace countdown | Not activatable; **decided (D4, 2026-08-25 — DEC-009):** 7-day grace at full tier grants with renewal notices, then fallback to the Free tier's grants at the **next session start** (FR-521/549); the fallback deactivates no device (FR-550) |
| SUSPENDED | Reversible hold (staff action; dunning once D2 lands) | **None → FR-504** (staff mutation; billing per D2) | "Suspended: {reason}" + resolution path | **Decided (D3, 2026-08-25 — DEC-010): soft.** Existing device tokens stay valid — the cascade must be scoped to exclude SUSPENDED (today it treats it exactly like REVOKED, making the status meaningless); new activations refused; manifest issuance and cloud/AI stopped; reinstatement returns to the prior status recorded at suspension time (FR-510) |
| REVOKED | Permanent kill (fraud, chargeback, abuse) | **None → FR-505** (staff mutation, reason mandatory) | "Revoked: contact support" | Not activatable; manifest issuance refused; tokens cascade-revoked ≤ 65 min; **cached offline entitlement persists until licence expiry** — the accepted DEC-004/005 trade-off (`apps/devices/tasks.py` docstring), restated in RISK-501 |
| CONVERTED | Superseded by a successor key (plan change / trial→paid) | **None → FR-506 (DEFERRED, D1)** | "Upgraded to {new plan}" with successor linked | Devices migrate to the successor key without re-activation |
| ARCHIVED | Terminal hygiene after retention post EXPIRED/REVOKED/CONVERTED | **None → FR-507** (scheduled) | Hidden from default views; visible in history | None (already inert) |

**`DeviceStatus`** (`apps/devices/models.py:4-7`):

| Status | Meaning | Writer (today → required) | Customer sees | Device effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACTIVE | Counted instance | Shipped: activation default (`models.py:35`) | Listed with name/platform/last-seen | Full service |
| REVOKED | Deactivated instance (slot freed) | **None → FR-511 (customer), FR-512 (staff)** — consumed today at `services.py:130,587` but never produced | Listed as deactivated | Token refused on next use; re-activation = normal activation (FR-513) |

**`DeviceTokenStatus`**: ACTIVE → REVOKED written by the cascade (`tasks.py:49`) and by re-mint supersession (`services.py:215-218`). Complete as-is; no new writer required.

**Legal transitions (exhaustive).** ISSUED→ACTIVATED (first activation) · ISSUED/ACTIVATED→EXPIRING (clock, FR-502) · EXPIRING→ACTIVATED (renewal, FR-509) · EXPIRING→EXPIRED (clock, FR-503) · EXPIRED→ACTIVATED (late renewal, FR-509; window per D1) · any non-terminal→SUSPENDED (FR-504) · SUSPENDED→its prior status (FR-510) · any→REVOKED (FR-505) · ACTIVATED/EXPIRING→CONVERTED (FR-506, D1) · EXPIRED/REVOKED/CONVERTED→ARCHIVED (FR-507). REVOKED and ARCHIVED are otherwise terminal. Any transition not listed is illegal and must be refused and audited.

## 15. Permissions

- **Customers** act only through `require_customer_org()` (DEC-007): every read/write is scoped to the caller's org; cross-org access is structurally impossible, not just refused. Org **Admin** may deactivate devices, view the licence/devices, and (post-D1/D2) manage billing; ordinary members get read-only visibility (Proposed, AS-P2). `seat_limit` enforcement stays dormant (NG-P4).
- **Staff** actions (issue, suspend, revoke, reinstate, renew, convert, deactivate, audit reads) require the ADMIN actor context on the admin GraphQL surface; every action records actor + reason (FR-508). Finer staff-role granularity (support-read-only vs operator) is R2.
- **Devices** authenticate with their device token only; a token can never re-mint itself (`services.py:195-201` invariant preserved).

Licence keys, devices and tokens are retained while the org is active; ARCHIVED (FR-507) after a Proposed 24-month post-terminal retention (AS-P2; final value with D1's commercial terms). Audit events are append-only and retained ≥ the licence's lifetime + retention window. Account data deletion/export follows the platform privacy obligations (§20, FR-176/177 v1.1 posture): deleting an org anonymises audit actor references rather than destroying the trail. Usage events (FR-532) carry no content; they are retained at least through their quota period plus the audit horizon — they substantiate FR-546 balances — before aging out. All schema changes remain forward-only (CON-P5).

## 17. AI and entitlement gating

The platform does not implement AI. Its AI-related duties: the entitlement manifest carries the plan's hosted-AI grants as typed values (FR-545 — per D1: STT 30 min / 5 h / 10 h by tier, values volatile and therefore data, FR-544), the platform records consumption and serves balances (FR-546), and the SelahCue cloud service (86ajy04hz) enforces them (FR-533), with exhaustion degrading transcription only (FR-547, FLOW-507). AI remains assistive and never gates core presentation (v1.1 FR-083/NFR-024); hosted-only, no BYOK (NG-P5); TTS stays out (DEC-001).

## 18. Offline behaviour

DEC-005, as amended by DEC-009 (D4, 2026-08-25), is the standing rule: the cached signed entitlement is valid for the **full licence window plus the 7-day renewal grace** — no other offline-grace timer. A device that activates once can run air-gapped to the end of the window (NFR-502). At the window's end the decided D4 applies: 7 days of grace at full grants, then Free-tier fallback at the next session start (FR-521/549/550, FLOW-505); in-session never-blank stands throughout (CON-P1). Refresh is opportunistic and silent (FR-519, NFR-504).

## 19. Security

Threats and controls: entitlement forgery → Ed25519 over the exact transmitted payload (CON-P7), never-unsigned posture, custody/rotation per the decided D5 (DEC-011; FR-518/538, RISK-502). Token theft → hashed at rest (FR-540), revocable (cascade + FR-511), never self-re-minting. Enumeration → CON-P6/FR-529, with D6 the only sanctioned carve-out. Abuse of staff surfaces → §15 actor gating + FR-508 audit. Webhook forgery → FR-537 signature + idempotency. Flooding → FR-539 throttling. Update integrity remains FR-155 (v1.1) — signed + anti-rollback, and FR-531 keeps security patches reachable in degraded licence states.

## 20. Privacy

Account and licensing data is customer business data, minimal by design: org, users' emails, device names/fingerprints, licence records — no sermon content, no congregation data, and usage events are content-free (FR-532). The published privacy policy + store disclosures (FR-176 v1.1) extend to the platform surfaces; DPA/cross-border handling (FR-177 v1.1) applies to the email-delivery and (post-D2) payment providers. Deletion/export per §16.

## 21. Accessibility

The customer portal and admin console meet the same bar the product holds elsewhere (v1.1 NFR-021/026 posture): keyboard-completable flows for activation, deactivation and renewal; WCAG AA contrast; licensing emails (FR-528) are plain-language and screen-reader friendly. A church admin must be able to complete FLOW-502 with a keyboard alone.

## 22. Reliability

Lifecycle jobs are idempotent, bounded and safe to re-run (NFR-505); beat dispatch is pinned by test (`tests/test_revocation_cascade.py` pattern). The platform's unavailability is a non-event for licensed desktops (NFR-504). Webhooks tolerate redelivery (FR-537). The cascade remains the backstop that converges token state to licence state within an hour (NFR-503).

## 23. Performance

Activation p95 ≤ 3 s, manifest p95 ≤ 1 s (NFR-507); throttling protects these under load (FR-539). Desktop-side: refresh is fully off the live path — slide-trigger latency (v1.1 METRIC-002) is unaffected by any licensing activity (FR-519 acceptance measures this).

## 24. Licensing terms and policy

What a licence grants (per the decided D1 tier structure): the org's right to run SelahCue on up to its tier's seat count of activated device instances (AS-P8) for the window `starts_at…expires_at` (followed by the D4 7-day renewal grace, then Free-tier fallback — DEC-009), the tier's screens/outputs and NDI output counts, its org-pooled monthly STT allowance (AS-P6/P7), watermark-free output on paid tiers, and updates within the window (feature updates gated by state per FR-531). These are owner-owned. This PRD states each with its options and consequences and maps the contingent requirements; **it decides none of them — the owner does.** Status as of v1.2 (2026-08-25): **D3, D4, D5 and D6 are DECIDED** (recorded below with the owner's rationale, folded into §13, and logged as DEC-009…DEC-012 in `docs/decisions/DECISION-LOG.md`); **D1 is PARTIALLY DECIDED** (DEC-008 — tier structure, seat unit and STT quota semantics; pricing and affiliate terms open); **D2 stays OPEN**.

**D1 — Commercial model** ([86ak10gph](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak10gph)): **PARTIALLY DECIDED (owner, 2026-08-25).** The plan/tier structure and entitlement limits are decided, verbatim in substance:

Owner, verbatim: "the names can change in the nearest future, the hrs for STT can also change as this is dependent on the AI subscription we start using" — which is why FR-544 makes the catalogue data and FR-545 keeps tier names out of the manifest's semantics. **In a same-day follow-up the owner also decided:** the seat unit — a seat is a device instance/activation under the org's **single** licence key, so "each with its own license" means its own device token, not its own `AppLicenseKey` (AS-P8; DEC-004 unchanged, matches the shipped code, no migration); the STT quota period — per calendar month, resetting on the billing anniversary (AS-P6); and the STT quota scope — pooled per org across all seats (AS-P7). **Released by these decisions:** FR-515/FR-516 (limit values and seat model), FR-533/FR-546 (grant, period and scope), and OQ-P1's framing (below, now a defect finding); FR-544–FR-548 were added to carry them. **Still open in D1:** pricing/price points; affiliate terms (the portal itself stays NG-P1); AS-P9 (what a screen/output counts); final STT hour values (tied to the AI-subscription choice); tier naming. *Still gates:* FR-506 (conversion/trial terms), FR-509 (renewal automation economics), FR-534, catalogue pricing content, METRIC-505's baseline.

**D2 — Payment provider + merchant of record** ([86ak10g47](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak10g47)): *Options:* Stripe (SelahCue is MoR — owns tax/VAT/refunds/dunning); Paddle or similar MoR (provider owns tax/refunds, less control, simpler compliance); FastSpring-class. *Consequences:* who owns tax/VAT and refunds (FR-536, §24), dunning cadence (FR-535), webhook shape (FR-537), invoice surface, FR-176/177 disclosure updates. *Gates:* all of EPIC-PL-F, FR-528's dunning notices.

**D3 — Should SUSPENDED kill device tokens as hard as REVOKED?** ([86ak10g8q](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak10g8q)): **DECIDED (owner, 2026-08-25 — DEC-010): soft suspend — SUSPENDED is softer than REVOKED, and reversible.** Running devices keep working (tokens stay valid); new activations are refused; cloud/AI features stop; reinstatement returns the licence to its prior status. Revocation stays the deliberate, terminal, token-killing act. *Principle:* a commercial problem degrades commercial surfaces, not the show — a suspension is a lever for a billing situation expected to resolve, and killing a church's presentation tokens over a failed card is disproportionate and hits during a service; revocation keeps its teeth. *Context (why this needed deciding):* today the two are operationally identical — both leave the activatable set (`services.py:40`), so the hourly cascade revokes tokens for both, making SUSPENDED meaningless as a distinct state. *Implementation notes recorded with the decision:* `SUSPENDED → prior status` means the prior status must be **recorded at suspension time** — a schema implication, not just a service one — or reinstatement cannot know where to return; the revocation cascade must be **scoped so SUSPENDED is not a token-killing state** (the code change lands with cascade correctness, 86ak1099u; reachability is 86ak5mn00's job); a suspended licence's already-cached offline entitlement keeps working until its own expiry — intended, RISK-501-consistent. *Was gating — now released:* FR-504, FR-510, FR-522, FLOW-506.

**D4 — Renewal grace: does a lapsed licence keep working?** ([86ak10ga1](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak10ga1)): **DECIDED (owner, 2026-08-25 — DEC-009, an amendment to DEC-005's no-separate-grace rule): a 7-day grace period, after which the subscription falls back to the FREE TIER — not a shutdown.** A lapsed licence never stops the app; after 7 days it degrades to Free (watermark, 1 device, 2 outputs, no NDI, 30 min STT). **Two rules are the substance of the decision, both normative:** **(1) the Free-tier fallback takes effect at the next session start** — a session already running finishes on the entitlement it began with (FR-549). Falling to Free changes four things a congregation can see, and if day 7 elapses at 10:40 on a Sunday all four would land mid-service; FR-548 already forbade exactly this for the watermark, and the identical rule now extends to the whole downgrade — grace expiry is a state change the console reflects immediately and the output path honours only at a session boundary. Without this rule, a 7-day grace merely delays a mid-service degradation by a week. **(2) A downgrade must not deactivate or revoke surplus devices** — when a 3-seat Pro org drops to Free's 1 device, all device records and tokens stay intact and the limit is enforced at session start: the first device to start a session gets it, the others are told why and offered the upgrade path; re-subscribing restores the org exactly as it was, with no re-activation and no burnt instance slots (FR-550; the rejected alternative destroys state on a billing event). *Was gating — now released:* FR-521, FLOW-505; FR-549/550 added to carry the two rules.

**D5 — Entitlement signing-key rotation + custody** ([86ak10gb9](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak10gb9)): **DECIDED (owner, 2026-08-25 — DEC-011), three parts deliberately split by cost and reversibility.** **(1) Custody: NO KMS at MVP** — the key stays in the hosting platform's encrypted secret store with a single documented holder. The accepted risk, recorded explicitly: whoever obtains the private key can mint unlimited entitlements at any tier, valid for any duration, entirely offline — no server call to rate-limit, no login to detect, no audit row, no way to know it happened; rotation is the only remedy, which is why part 2 is not optional. **Revisit trigger: first revenue, or the first N paying orgs — whichever comes first** — a deferral with a named end, not a permanent position; it enters the launch security review (86ak11w10) as an accepted-risk item rather than being discovered there as a finding. **(2) Rotation: the client trusts a KEY SET from the first shipped build — not deferrable.** The public key is compiled into every installed copy; a single-key client means any future key change rejects every new entitlement everywhere at once, unfixable by shipping an update because the machines that matter are deliberately offline. The client holds a set of trusted public keys; the manifest carries a `key_id`; the documented rotation procedure is add → ship → adopt → switch signing → retire after the overlap window. Cost: an array instead of a constant, plus one field — and it is what makes part 1 a safe deferral. **(3) Activation: account login is the primary path; the enrolment key is retained as delegation — no code change** (already DEC-004's model, both paths ship). Removing the key path was considered and rejected: it deletes tested code, and it costs the volunteer-installer case — a volunteer setting up sound-booth machines would otherwise need the admin's account password on each one. *Scope bar:* signing raises the attack cost from "edit a JSON file" to "patch and re-sign a binary"; it does not defeat an attacker who controls the machine, and no custody scheme would — build to that bar, not beyond it. *Was gating — now released:* FR-518, FR-538.

**D6 — Distinct EXPIRED error code, or keep it collapsed into VALIDATION_FAILED?** ([86ak120fz](https://app.clickup.com/t/86ak120fz)): **DECIDED (owner, 2026-08-25 — DEC-012): split the answer by surface rather than answering it once.** **Licence/entitlement state → expose `EXPIRED` explicitly** (FR-523): no enumeration oracle exists there — a church told "your licence expired" learns nothing about anyone else's account, and collapsing it into VALIDATION_FAILED only generates support load. **Auth tokens (`verify_email`, `confirm_password_reset`) → keep the collapse**: unknown / consumed / expired / wrong-purpose stay indistinguishable; FR-529's anti-enumeration carve-out stands, and the independent security review that established this behaviour was right to want it. **Plus two fixes that leak nothing:** (a) **validate the token BEFORE the password — a live defect verified in the tree**: `apps/accounts/services.py:968` calls `_validate_password()` before the token lookup at lines 973–987, both raising the same `_validation_error()`, so a valid link plus a weak password is indistinguishable from a dead link and the user loops forever — FR-551, with a regression test pinning the order; (b) `/verify` and `/reset` must **always** offer "request a new link", whatever the error, since the API deliberately cannot say why a token failed — FR-552 (`resendVerificationEmail` already ships, 86ak120ac). Together these end the motivating scenarios — a verification email in spam past its 24h TTL, a reset link clicked after its 1h TTL — in "request a new one", not a dead end. *Was gating — now released:* FR-523, FR-529; FR-551/552 added.

**OQ-P1 (revisited under the owner's seat decision):** the dual `device_limit`. Since an org holds a **single** licence key (AS-P8), the current behaviour — an org's effective capacity being the **sum of its keys' limits** when it holds several (`apps/devices/services.py:290,416` enforces per key only) — is a **genuine defect**, not the intended seat design. Two things remain open: (a) `CustomerOrg.device_limit` (`apps/accounts/models.py:25`) needs either a defined job or removal; (b) what a multi-key org even means (a second campus? a historic key awaiting CONVERTED?) is an open modelling question — but the sum-of-limits behaviour is wrong under every candidate answer and must not be relied on. FR-515 documents the enforced per-key truth meanwhile. Disposition owner: product owner, with the D1 pricing remainder.
